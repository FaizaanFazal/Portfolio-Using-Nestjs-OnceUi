## The question

Does APOE ε4 genotype predict cognitive decline on its own, or does its effect depend on how much hippocampal volume a patient has already lost?

## What I did

Modeled cognitive decline trajectories against APOE ε4 status and hippocampal volume jointly, testing for an interaction effect rather than treating them as independent predictors.

## Result

The two interact: ε4 carriers with lower hippocampal volume decline faster than either factor alone would predict.

## What it means

Risk models for cognitive decline should combine genetic and structural markers rather than scoring them separately — the combination carries more information than the sum of its parts.
