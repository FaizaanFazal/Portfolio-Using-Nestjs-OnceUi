## The question

Can a 3D U-Net segment the hippocampus from MRI as accurately as heavier architectures, without their memory and compute footprint?

## What I did

Built a 3D U-Net with two preprocessing additions: CLAHE (contrast-limited adaptive histogram equalization) to normalize MRI contrast, and SCE-3DWT, a custom 3D wavelet-transform-based preprocessing step, to reduce input dimensionality before the network ever sees the volume. Segmentation quality is scored with the Dice coefficient, which measures the overlap between the predicted mask $X$ and the ground-truth mask $Y$:

$$DSC = \frac{2|X \cap Y|}{|X| + |Y|}$$

## What it means

Resource-constrained clinical settings — a lab without a GPU cluster — don't have to choose between segmentation accuracy and being able to run the model at all.
