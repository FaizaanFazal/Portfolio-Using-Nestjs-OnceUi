## The question

Most AD classifiers collapse the disease into a binary label. Can a single model stage patients across six clinically meaningful severity levels from structural MRI alone?

## What I did

Built a multi-scale 3D CNN that processes MRI volumes at multiple resolutions simultaneously, trained with imbalance-aware losses to handle the fact that middle disease stages are far less represented than the endpoints.

## Result

Six-class staging directly from structural MRI, evaluated with metrics that don't reward the model for defaulting to the majority classes.

## What it means

Finer-grained staging is what a longitudinal care plan actually needs — knowing roughly where on the AD spectrum a patient sits changes what follow-up looks like, not just whether they have the disease.
