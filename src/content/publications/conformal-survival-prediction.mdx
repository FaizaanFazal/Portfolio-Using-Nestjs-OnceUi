## The question

A clinician wants more than a single predicted date of conversion from MCI to Alzheimer's — they want an honest uncertainty range, and one that doesn't quietly assume follow-up data is complete.

## What I did

Applied conformal prediction to MCI-to-AD survival modeling, calibrating explicitly for two things standard survival models ignore: missingness in follow-up visits, and baseline symptom severity.

## What it means

Uncertainty-aware prediction is what makes a survival model usable in a clinical conversation — a range a clinician can act on, with a stated confidence, rather than a number presented as certain when it isn't.
