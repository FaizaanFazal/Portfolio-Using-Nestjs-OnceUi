## The question

A classifier can flag a scan as "likely Alzheimer's" — but will a clinician trust it without knowing why?

## What I did

Combined an EfficientNetV2B0 classifier for MRI-based Alzheimer's diagnosis with explainability techniques (Grad-CAM, LIME, SHAP) to surface which regions of the scan the model weighted most heavily.

## Result

The model's attention consistently localized to regions already associated with AD pathology (hippocampus, medial temporal lobe), giving a visual, region-level check on whether the classifier is reasoning about the right anatomy.

## What it means

Explainability isn't a compliance checkbox — it's how a radiologist decides whether to trust a specific prediction, on a specific scan, not just the model's aggregate accuracy.
