## The question

Do the preprocessing choices that improve hippocampal segmentation on one MRI dataset transfer to another, or does each dataset need its own recipe?

## What I did

Ran a systematic ablation study over common preprocessing steps (normalization, contrast enhancement, resampling) across multiple hippocampal segmentation datasets, then tested transfer learning between them.

## Result

No single preprocessing pipeline was universally best — the optimal choice was dataset-dependent, and transfer learning performance degraded when preprocessing assumptions didn't match between source and target datasets.

## What it means

Papers reporting segmentation results should treat preprocessing as a variable worth ablating, not a fixed pipeline copied from prior work — what works on ADNI won't automatically work on a different acquisition protocol.
